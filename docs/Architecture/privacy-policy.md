# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026

This policy describes the current university-project implementation of Gaffer. It is not a statement of commercial-production compliance or a substitute for formal legal advice.

## 1. Who we are and scope

Gaffer is a university/student project that supports sport-coaching activities. For purposes of South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (“POPIA”), the project team is the responsible party for personal information processed through the Service. The contact details for the responsible party are: [2801261@students.wits.ac.za](mailto:2801261@students.wits.ac.za).

## 3. How and why we process information

We process information directly from users and coaches, from Google if you choose that sign-in method, and automatically through authentication/session operation. The purposes are to provide the requested functionality, verify email addresses, authenticate and secure access, keep team data separated by team, respond to requests, maintain the Service and meet legal obligations where applicable.

Under POPIA, processing is undertaken as necessary to provide the Service requested by users, for the project team’s legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, with consent where appropriate, or where otherwise permitted or required by law. We aim to collect only information relevant to these purposes and ask users to keep it accurate.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

Gaffer uses essential authentication cookies set by its authentication system to keep a signed-in session. They are configured with a `SameSite=Lax` setting and, in production, a secure-cookie setting. The frontend sends these cookies with requests to the Service so authenticated features can work.

The Service also uses browser local storage for a light/dark theme choice and, temporarily, to preserve a pending team-invitation or athlete-claim token through the email-verification flow. We found no analytics, advertising pixels, or other non-essential tracking scripts in the application code. You may clear local storage in your browser, but doing so can interrupt those convenience features.

## 5. Service providers and disclosures

The deployed configuration and code use or provide for the following third parties: Vercel for the frontend, Render for the backend deployment, Neon/PostgreSQL for the application database, Google for optional OAuth sign-in, and Brevo for transactional email verification when its email service is configured. Better Auth is the authentication software used within the backend. These providers may process relevant information to provide their services. We do not sell personal information and do not use the Service for direct marketing.

## 6. International transfers

Hosting, database, authentication and email providers may process information outside South Africa, depending on their infrastructure and your use of Google sign-in. Where a cross-border transfer occurs, we intend to use providers that are subject to appropriate contractual, technical or legal safeguards, as required by POPIA. The project does not represent that all processing occurs in South Africa.

## 7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated sessions, email verification, team-scoped authorisation, password handling through the authentication system, and hashed storage for one-time invitation tokens. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Do not share credentials or invitation links. If you suspect unauthorised access, contact us promptly.

## 8. Retention

The current application has no published automatic retention schedule or self-service account-deletion feature. Personal information and associated team records may remain in the database while the account or project data remains available, unless a deletion request is actioned, deletion follows from a related record being removed, or retention is no longer necessary. We will review retention where reasonably practical for this university project.

## 9. Your rights and requests

Subject to POPIA and applicable limitations, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or ask about the information held about you. The profile interface currently allows a signed-in user to update their name, phone number, sex and date of birth. It does not currently support changing an email address, password reset/change, or self-service account deletion. For other requests, email [2801261@students.wits.ac.za](mailto:2801261@students.wits.ac.za); we may need to verify your identity and will assess the request in light of technical and legal requirements.

## 10. Children and athlete data

The Service may contain athlete information, including date of birth, entered by coaches. It is not designed for children to independently provide personal information without appropriate adult involvement. Coaches must ensure they have the required authority, consent or other lawful basis before entering or sharing a child’s information. If you believe a child’s information has been submitted improperly, contact us.

## 11. Security incidents, updates and complaints

We will assess suspected security incidents and, where required by POPIA, notify affected people and the Information Regulator. We may update this policy as the project evolves; the effective date above will show when it was last revised.

You may lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator. Current contact details should be confirmed from the regulator’s official website before use: [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za/).

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact [2801261@students.wits.ac.za](mailto:2801261@students.wits.ac.za).
