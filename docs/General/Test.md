## 1   Overview


## 1.1   Projects & Groups

Each project is broken down into three tiers: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced. To achieve the basic tier,

your project needs to meet all the listed key and basic requirements, as well as achieve the standard of a

minimum viable product . The intermediate tier needs to meet all the listed basic requirements and the

intermediate requirements. The expected standard is that of a functional and useful product. The

advanced tier needs to meet   all   listed requirements, even those potentially introduced part way through

the semester. The standard of an advanced project is one that can be released to market the day of

submission.

You can find a brief problem statement for each project in this document, but additional information will

be provided through project specific briefs.

Groups are 4 - 6 members. Please select your group carefully as you may not change your group once

the semester has started.

Your group will be assigned a tutor as a ‘client’. Their role is to help guide you on the development

process and make decisions around if a feature is ‘complete’ or not. Your tutor will act as the first point

of contact for any questions about your project, but the lecturer will be available if you would like

further clarification. You will   not   be marked by your client, but by other tutors. The client is however

expected to provide some context to the marking tutor on your interactions with them.


## 1.2   Milestones & Reports


## 1.2.1   Milestone 1: Sprint 1

Milestone 1 is aimed at project setup and the basics of a project. Setting up the project repositories,

getting the largest ‘blockers’ out of the way, making sure you and your group have a strong direction for

the rest of the semester.


## 1.2.2   Milestone 2: Sprint 2

Milestone 2 is the midpoint of the project, where by the end of this sprint you have a strong base for the

project. The most important features are implemented, even if there are issues, and the project is

moving towards a stable product through testing and reviews.


## 1.2.3   Milestone 3: Sprint 3

Milestone 3 is aimed at getting the project into a near-complete state. Most features should be

implemented, and mostly working. The project should have gone through reviews and you should be

evaluating the state of the project from a qualitative perspective.


## 1.2.4   Milestone 4: Submission

Milestone 4 is the final submission. Your project should work, work well, and do what it needs to.
